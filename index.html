Who We Are

A South African holding company built on reliability

Lembrose Holding Company is a proudly South African business focused on general trading, procurement and logistics. We connect businesses, government entities and project teams with the goods they need, and we make sure those goods arrive where they're needed, when they're needed.

The company is owned and led by Bhekisisa Dlamini (Owner & Director).

Our model is simple: understand the requirement, source it from vetted suppliers, manage the logistics end to end, and keep the client informed at every step.

Our Mission

To deliver dependable trading and logistics solutions that help our clients operate without interruption.

Our Vision

To be a trusted name in African trade and supply-chain services, known for integrity and consistency.

Our Values

Integrity · Accountability · Efficiency · Partnership
